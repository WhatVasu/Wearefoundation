Reunification

Reunited After 5 Years on the Streets

Situation: A man lost his memory and home after a severe accident, spending 5 long years living on the streets without support. Intervention: Our team provided immediate shelter, food, and care, and launched a social media search campaign to locate his relatives. Outcome: The post reached his hometown in Karnataka, helping us successfully trace his family and safely reunite him back home. Shared with consent; identifying details withheld.

Dignified Last Rites

Dignified Last Rites

Situation: A father was left waiting at a cremation ground for eight hours with his deceased disabled son, unable to afford the final rites. Intervention: Upon learning of his plight, our team arrived at the location to provide immediate emotional and financial support. Outcome: We performed the final rites with full respect and dignity, relieving the grieving father of his burden. Shared with consent; identifying details withheld.

Child Protection

Rescuing a Child in Danger

Situation: A child was found in an unsafe environment with parents who were unconscious due to extreme alcohol intoxication. Intervention: Working together with Childline, our team intervened immediately to rescue the child from harm. Outcome: The child was safely placed in a children's home, ensuring their immediate protection, proper care, and well-being. Shared with consent; identifying details withheld.

Empowerment & Employment

Empowering a Visually Impaired Resident

Situation: In Boyapally village (Mahabubnagar), Mr. Raju, who is visually impaired, was facing financial difficulties and lacked a sustainable livelihood. Intervention: Our team provided him with financial assistance and created an employment opportunity tailored to his abilities. Outcome: He gained self-reliance, restored his confidence, and is now able to lead an independent and dignified life. Shared with consent; identifying details withheld.

Our Story

Why We Are Foundation exists

Our work started in 2018. We saw people on the street suffering with mental health issues—wandering hungry, cold, and drenched in the rain. It broke our hearts, and we knew we could not just look away. What began as a small effort to give food, shelter, and care soon grew into a bigger mission. In 2023, we officially registered as WE ARE FOUNDATION (Charitable Trust) (Reg. No: BK IV-4/2023).

Today we work alongside families, volunteers, mental health professionals, and community partners to close that gap — through outreach, coordination, and steady, patient support.

Mission

To assist NGOs, volunteers, police, and families in locating and reuniting missing persons quickly and safely.

Vision

To create a secure and reliable platform that helps reunite missing, vulnerable persons with their families, and to work toward a beggar-free city through technology and community support.
